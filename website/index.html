AI Disclosure

Jesus Interactive uses artificial intelligence to generate responses in the voice and character of Jesus Christ, grounded in Scripture and shaped by a persona we designed. It is a simulation for personal reflection, encouragement, and spiritual growth.

It is not Jesus Himself, not a member of clergy, not a licensed therapist or counselor, and not a medical or legal professional. It cannot forgive sins, perform sacraments, or replace your relationship with a local church, pastor, or faith community.

Responses are AI-generated and may occasionally be incomplete or imperfect despite our best efforts to ground them in Scripture. If you are in crisis, please contact your local emergency number or a crisis line immediately — this app is not a substitute for emergency or professional care.
